1. Introduction

Fotak ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by RazTech (S Raju), is a free civic engagement platform designed for citizens across India to report local civic issues (roads, drainage, water supply, streetlights, garbage, electricity, etc.) with verified geotagged photos and videos, track resolution progress with local authorities, and foster community civic transparency.

We prioritize user privacy and transparency. This Privacy Policy discloses our practices concerning the collection, use, protection, and disclosure of information when you download, install, and use the Fotak mobile application.

Core Privacy Commitment: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertising companies or data brokers. Data collected is strictly used to verify civic complaints and support public civic resolution.

2. Information We Collect and Permissions Used

To provide core civic reporting functionalities, the Fotak app accesses and collects the following categories of data:

A. Phone Number & Authentication Data

  • Data Collected: Mobile Phone Number.
  • Purpose: For one-time SMS verification (OTP) powered by Google Firebase Phone Authentication to verify that complaints and citizen votes originate from authentic Indian mobile numbers, preventing spam and automated bots.
  • Read-Only Access: Browsing civic issues in the app does not require a phone number (users can browse as guests). Phone verification is only requested when filing a complaint, voting, or creating community posts.

B. Precise & Approximate Location Data (GPS)

  • Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION.
  • Purpose: Geotagging civic issues with precise latitude, longitude, ward, and locality so municipal authorities and local volunteers know the exact geographic spot of the complaint.
  • Usage Mode: Location is accessed only in the foreground while you are creating a report or picking a complaint location on the map. We do not track your background location.

C. Photos, Videos, and Audio (Camera & Media Storage)

  • Permissions: CAMERA, RECORD_AUDIO.
  • Purpose: Capturing live photos and videos of physical civic issues and optional voice notes explaining local problems.
  • Storage: Uploaded media files are stored securely on Cloudflare R2 object storage via secure, time-limited presigned URLs.

D. Device Identifiers & Push Notification Data

  • Data Collected: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Device Token.
  • Purpose: Sending timely notifications regarding status updates on your filed complaints, authority replies, and important local civic alerts.

E. Crash Reports & App Performance Data

  • Service: Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Data Collected: Crash traces, device model, operating system version, and general error logs to diagnose app crashes and ensure stability. No personally identifiable credentials are logged in crash traces.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Authenticating citizen accounts via SMS OTP.
  • Publishing civic complaints on the public feed and mapping them to specific municipal wards.
  • Providing aggregated statistics and transparent governance metrics (e.g. Ward Scorecards).
  • Enabling authorized government representatives to verify and update resolution milestones.
  • Ensuring platform integrity by filtering fake, defamatory, abusive, or duplicate submissions.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry-standard cloud infrastructure providers who adhere to strict data privacy and security standards:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, FCM, Crashlytics): Used for secure SMS OTP authentication, push notifications, and crash diagnostics. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase (PostgreSQL Database): Cloud-hosted database utilizing Row Level Security (RLS) to enforce data access controls. (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare R2: Encrypted, highly available cloud object storage for photos and video evidence. (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)

5. Financial Transactions & Voluntary Support

The Fotak app provides an optional "App Support" feature where users can voluntarily contribute funds to support server and maintenance costs via standard Indian UPI payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).

  • Transactions take place directly within your chosen third-party UPI app via standard upi:// intent.
  • Fotak does not collect, process, or store bank account details, debit/credit card numbers, or UPI PINs.

6. Data Retention, User Rights, and Account Deletion

We believe you have complete control over your personal data. You are entitled to the following rights:

  • Access & Review: View all complaints, votes, and activities associated with your account under the Profile screen.
  • In-App Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated personal records at any time directly inside the app:
    Settings → Account Section → Delete Account.
  • Email Deletion Request: You can submit a deletion request by sending an email from your registered contact to fotakofficial@gmail.com with the subject "Fotak Account Deletion Request". Upon receipt, all associated personal identifiers are permanently scrubbed from our active databases within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The Fotak application is designed for citizens aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can remove such information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services or legal obligations. We will notify users of any substantial changes by posting the revised policy on this page with an updated "Effective Date" and through an in-app announcement.
